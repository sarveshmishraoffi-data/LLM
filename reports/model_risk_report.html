3. Responsible AI & Fair Lending Audit (ECOA)

Protected attributes (Gender and Age Group) were isolated from training inputs and tested strictly during independent validation to verify non-discrimination under the 80% Four-Fifths rule.

  • Gender Disparity: Disparate Impact Ratio satisfies compliance guidelines with no statistically significant approval divergence.
  • Age Demographics: Tested across Young (<25), Working Age (25-55), and Senior (>55). Model shows calibrated risk assessment across all cohorts.

4. Explainability & Adverse Action Notices (FCRA)

Using SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations), each applicant decision is deconstructed into explicit positive and negative risk drivers. Top adverse action drivers identified globally are: (1) Debt-to-Income Ratio, (2) Historical Default Records, and (3) Trailing 24-Month Delinquencies.

5. GenAI / LLM Evaluation & Governance

The generative risk analysis component was evaluated against four core vulnerability vectors:

  • Prompt Sensitivity: Tested across strict, standard, and lenient prompt formulations. Decision consistency rate demonstrated acceptable operational bounds.
  • Hallucination Detection: Assessed whether LLM invented derogatory items (e.g. bankruptcies, liens). Groundedness score remained high across audited samples.
  • Safety Guardrails: Prohibited demographic keywords were audited; the LLM refrained from referencing age, gender, or marital status in credit decisions.

6. Model Risk Officer (MRO) Final Recommendations

  1. Deployment Verdict: Conditional Approval granted for production pilot.
  2. Monitoring Protocol: Implement automated weekly Population Stability Index (PSI) tracking; retrain when PSI exceeds 0.25.
  3. GenAI Deployment Control: Pin LLM temperature to 0.0 in live environments and enforce deterministic schema validation.
